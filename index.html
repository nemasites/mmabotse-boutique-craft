The House

Mission

At Mmabotse Boutique Craft, our mission is to provide individuals who value the artistry of handmade items with exceptional products that evoke a sense of warmth, style, and individuality. We are dedicated to cultivating a deep sense of community and passion for crochet, fostering connections with our customers, and delivering unparalleled craftsmanship and personalised service.

Craftsmanship & Quality

Mmabotse takes immense pride in the craftsmanship of each item, ensuring meticulous attention to detail and using only the finest materials — including snow Strom, 100% wool yarn and woven maracame. Lerato's dedication to excellence shines through in every stitch, resulting in products that are both visually stunning and durable.

Product Range

Winter Ready-to-Wear: hats, jerseys, cardigans and coats, designed to keep customers warm and stylish through the cold months.

Handbags: made using maracame, woven yarn and other fabrics — functional and fashionable, for everyday use or as a statement piece.
